Before good design, I look for the design that fits people.

Complexity itself isn’t the problem.What we need to remove is the complexity that confuses people.

I keep necessary complexity, and reduce the uncertainty that makes people hesitate.

With AI, I design experiences where simplicity and confidence carry through the whole service.
